Framework

Five questions before an AI workflow ships

  1. OwnerWho is accountable for the result?
  2. BaselineHow does the work perform today?
  3. DataIs the data ready, permitted, and trustworthy?
  4. VerificationWhere does a human check the output?
  5. MeasureWhat number should move, and by when?

Proposed framework by Rudi Mohamed. Opinion, not a standard.
