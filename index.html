Point of view

Technology is not the outcome. What it enables is.

Most organizations don't have a technology problem. They have a gap between technology activity and measurable value: projects delivered, licenses bought, pilots launched, and too little change in what the institution can actually do.

For more than 20 years, from writing and integrating software at Microsoft to leading technology for a regulated public financial institution, my work has been closing that gap: connecting strategy to execution, investment to value, innovation to real work, and governance to responsible outcomes.

How I think about value

From technology to outcomes

Every investment should be traceable along this chain. If a link is missing, the value is too.

  1. TechnologyPlatforms, data, AI, and the systems we build or buy.
  2. CapabilityWhat people and teams can now do that they couldn't before.
  3. Operational changeWorkflows, decisions, and roles redesigned to use it.
  4. OutcomeMeasurable mission, financial, and risk results.
